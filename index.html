How this game works

This is a learning game by Coded Female. Answers and feedback are written in advance. No AI model evaluates your choices, and no AI service receives your prompts. There can be several good ways to phrase a prompt. The game rewards the three details that best match each fictional task.

Everything runs in your browser. The game does not save your answers or prompt drafts, and does not use analytics or cookies. Reloading resets your progress. Downloads and copying happen only when you choose them.

AI can make mistakes even with a clear prompt. Check important claims and protect personal, team and customer information. Find more practical guidance on the Coded Female blog.
